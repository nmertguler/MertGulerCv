A Bit About Me

I have been developing games since 2021. I have taken part in every stage of game development, and I try to stay involved in all of them. My goal is to become a good game developer. I love developing games and trying new things.

I prefer managing both the technical and creative sides together. While coding, I think about design; while designing, I think about code. This cross-disciplinary perspective is the most important element shaping my games.
